### Must-fix

1. **idempotency**: the example puts an external card charge "in one transaction" with the key save. That can't work: a crash after the charge leads to a double charge on retry, the very bug the article is about. `[TA PS NR]`
2. **backpressure**: the timeout trap states the rule backwards. The real trap is an upstream timeout *shorter* than the downstream one. `[TA, NR flagged it as confusing]`
3. **cap-theorem**: "primary database plus replicas" is presented as escaping the trade-off, but replica reads are stale. `[PS must-fix, TA should-fix]`
4. **dry**: the headline tax-rate example de-duplicates *across services*, then the article says not to do that. `[PS]`

### Cross-cutting themes

- **Undefined jargon, mostly in Example sections.** Unexpanded acronyms (ACID, GC, SRE, RFC, LGTM, PCI, PR, ADR, p99) and terms used without a definition (jitter, bulkhead, CDC, projector, partitioning key, error budget, cardinality, semantic lock, fake vs mock, expand-and-contract, eventual consistency). `[NR SE]`
- **Examples that show only the happy path**, or show only half the code: separation-of-concerns (fixed version is prose only), code-review (no code under review), TDD (the refactor is never shown), idempotency, hexagonal, CQRS, DDD, modular-monolith. `[NR PS TA SE]`
- **Benefits stated without their limits**: replaceability (SoC, hexagonal), instant rollback (flags), paved roads, "change freely" (coupling), "stop anytime" (strangler). `[PS]`
- **Missing attributions**: PACELC/Abadi, Hickey, Brooks, Metz, Fowler's YAGNI costs, Bezos doors, Postel, Feathers/SOLID, the Raymond compiler quote, Team Topologies, Cohn's pyramid, Meyer's CQS. `[TA]`
- **Sibling articles that define a shared idea differently**: the rule of three vs. abstracting at the second case, "command", "core" (FCIS vs hexagonal), request ID vs idempotency key, CAP used for async staleness. `[WG SE]`
- **House-style drift**: attribution-first openings, inconsistent bold lead-ins on bullets, two different acronym title patterns, "trace/correlation/request ID", "group by domain/feature". `[SE]`
